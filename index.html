About

Starting at The Third Floor gave me a unique view into film production. With quick turn arounds, blockbuster requirements, and a team of artists who needed realtime tools; years ahead of the VFX industry. Since then I've led teams and pipelines through feature, episodic, and real-time productions.

My work usually sits at the intersection of three things: artist workflow, data integrity, and production deadlines. I write tools that artists actually use, document them so they survive without me, and design systems with more than the current project in sight.
